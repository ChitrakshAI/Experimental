## 1.9 Profile photo

**Founder headshot. Not a brand mark.**

Three reasons. First, no first-party Oraya or Hanflux logo file exists in either repository, so a "brand mark" would have to be made today and would look made today. Second, the entire positioning is one named accountable person with a law degree; a logo hides the only asset the competitors cannot copy. Third, on a zero-review account a logo reads as an agency reselling offshore labour, which is the read the $450 floor exists to defeat.

Specification:
- Shoulders-up, face filling roughly 60% of the frame, direct eye contact, square crop, minimum 250 x 250 px, shot at higher resolution and downscaled.
- Background: flat dark, close to the locked brand ink `#06070C` or `#0B0E17`. No office, no bookshelf, no gradient.
- Collared shirt or plain dark knit. No sunglasses, no headset, no text overlay, no logo lockup, no border.
- Neutral to slightly serious expression. This is a compliance product; a wide smile undercuts it.
- Use the same photo on LinkedIn. Buyers who find the reels and then the profile need the two faces to match instantly.
